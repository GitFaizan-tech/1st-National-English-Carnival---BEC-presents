Welcome to the Carnival

A platform where young minds come together to celebrate English through exciting challenges and creative experiences.

About the Carnival

The 1st Brightian National English Carnival is an initiative of Bright English Club (BEC) and Bright School and College. It brings together students to explore language, teamwork, critical thinking and creativity.

Our Mission

To encourage students to express themselves confidently, discover new abilities, work together and develop a lasting love for the English language.
